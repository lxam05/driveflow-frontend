Raheny Driving Test Routes – What to Expect, Common Mistakes & Local Tips

1. Narrow residential roads & parked cars

Raheny has lots of tight streets with cars parked on both sides. You're constantly judging priority. Failing to yield properly or squeezing through when you shouldn't costs marks.

2. Observation at junctions

Many minor junctions have limited visibility. Full stop where required, edge out slowly, and make your head movement obvious. Rolling or guessing is a common fail.

3. Speed control (very easy to drift)

Low limits dominate, and the roads feel wider than they are. Drifting over the limit, especially after turns, racks up faults quickly.

4. Manoeuvres in cramped areas

Reverse around a corner or turnabout is very likely and often done where space is tight. Kerbing, poor steering, or missed blind-spot checks show immediately.

5. Traffic flow & hesitation

Traffic can be stop-start. Over-hesitating at junctions or blocking traffic unnecessarily gets marked down. You need to be calm and decisive.

6. Positioning on busier roads

Some routes briefly hit wider or busier roads. Late lane choice or drifting within the lane stands out fast.

Bottom line

Raheny fails people on observation, priority judgment, and speed discipline. It's subtle, not dramatic. If your scanning and positioning aren't automatic, the area exposes it quickly.

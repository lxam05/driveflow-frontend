Booking the test
[ ] Confirm your preferred test centre and available dates via the Road Safety Authority (RSA) online system.
[ ] Ensure your 12-lesson EDT certificate is logged and visible in your RSA profile.
[ ] Check that your learner permit is valid (not expired) and you still meet all eligibility criteria.
[ ] Pay the test fee (or have payment method ready) as required by the RSA.
[ ] Receive and record your test appointment confirmation (date, time, test centre).
[ ] Check for any specific test centre instructions (arrival time, parking, signage).

The day(s) before
[ ] Review the driving test marking sheet and familiarise yourself with what the tester will evaluate.
[ ] Drive the route area of your test centre at least once, preferably at the same day/time of day.
[ ] Ensure your practice driving (in your car or instructor’s) is focused on the key fail areas: observation, road position, progress, mirrors/signals, speed, right-of-way.
[ ] Do at least one full mock test with your instructor under test-conditions.
[ ] Check your vehicle is fully legal: tax, NCT (if required), insurance, all lights working, tyres good tread.

Things to bring on test day
[ ] Your current learner permit (valid and not expired).
[ ] Photographic ID if required by your test centre (passport or driver learner permit).
[ ] Proof of payment of the test fee, or confirmation number if online.
[ ] The car’s insurance document, tax disc (if applicable), NCT certificate (if applicable).
[ ] The vehicle being used must display L-plates (front and back), in good condition.
[ ] Comfortable clothes and shoes (not awkward to drive in).
[ ] Arrive at least 15 minutes early at the test centre.
[ ] Make sure you have full view through windscreen/windows, mirrors properly set, seat adjusted, and ready to go.

On arrival & just before the test starts
[ ] Set up your seat, steering wheel and mirrors before the examiner arrives.
[ ] Complete the “start-up checklist”: seatbelt on, handbrake set, gear in neutral (or park if auto), ensure surrounding traffic is safe.
[ ] Take a moment to breathe, focus, and remind yourself you know what you’re doing.
[ ] The examiner will ask for you to do an eyesight test and maybe show your permit — be ready.
[ ] Listen clearly to the examiner’s instructions. Ask for clarification if you don’t understand something.

During the test
[ ] Remember: safe and consistent driving beats perfect.
[ ] Mirror → Signal → Position → Move: make this your automatic sequence.
[ ] Don’t rush. Make decisions confidently but avoid hesitating too long.
[ ] If you make a mistake: keep calm, correct it safely, and move on. One error doesn’t mean fail if corrected properly.
[ ] Finish every manoeuvre with mirrors and signal where required.

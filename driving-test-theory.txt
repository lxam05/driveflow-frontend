What should you do if you’re dazzled by oncoming headlights?
Look toward the left verge, ease off your speed, and stop if you can’t see safely.

When may you use your horn?
Only to warn others of a danger or to make them aware of your presence for safety.

What does a green arrow at traffic lights indicate?
You may go only in the direction shown by the arrow.

Who is not allowed to use a motorway?
Learner permit holders without a full licence, vehicles incapable of 50 km/h, and pedestrians.

What is the correct position for a right turn?
Right side of your lane.

What tyre tread depth is legally required?
1.6 mm minimum.

How would you recognise a Zebra crossing at night?
By the flashing amber beacons.

What do double yellow lines mean?
No parking at any time.

What do you do if the car behind is dazzling you in your mirror?
Tilt the interior mirror to anti-glare mode.

When can you cross a continuous white line?
Never unless for emergency or access.

What should you do if you see children or animals near the road?
Check mirrors, slow down, and move out slightly if needed.

What is a clearway?
A zone where stopping or parking is banned during stated hours.

What does a flashing amber pedestrian light mean?
You may proceed but must give way to pedestrians already crossing or waiting.

At a STOP sign with no stop line, where must you stop?
At the sign itself — do not pass it.

What does a red triangle placed on the road usually indicate?
A breakdown or hazard ahead.

What are the normal motorway lane rules?
Drive in the left lane unless overtaking or allowing traffic to merge.

How close may you park to a junction?
At least 5 metres away.

What is the maximum distance you can park from the kerb?
About half a metre (roughly 3 ft).

What should you do if traffic lights are not working?
Give way to traffic already turning and to vehicles on your right.

What situations require dipped headlights only?
When meeting/following traffic, in lit-up areas, in fog/snow, or sometimes during the day for safety.

When can you overtake on the left?
When the vehicle ahead is turning right, when turning left yourself and the right lane is slow/stopped, or in separate lanes with different flows.

What does a broken white line across your lane mean?
It’s a yield line — you must give way to traffic on the road you’re entering, including roundabouts.

How do you position yourself on a one-way street for a right turn?
Move to the right-hand side of the street.

What does a broken yellow line mean?
It marks the edge of the road, also known as the hard shoulder.

What do single yellow lines mean?
Parking is restricted during certain hours.

What does a broken white line in the centre of a road mean?
Overtaking is allowed when safe.

What do two broken lines in the road centre indicate?
A continuous white line is approaching ahead.

What do horizontal solid white markings in the centre of the road mean?
Treat them like a traffic island — do not drive into them.

Where should you never park?
Near bends, brows of hills, humpback bridges, continuous white lines, blocking signs, at bus stops, entrances, taxi ranks, or opposite an obstruction.

Where should you not overtake?
On bends, at junctions, on brows of hills, at humpback bridges, alongside continuous white lines, or anywhere with poor visibility.

What does an amber traffic light mean?
Prepare to stop unless stopping would cause danger or you cannot stop safely before the line.

What is the max speed limit for a learner driver?
100 km/h.

What are the general speed limits?
50 km/h in built-up areas, 80 km/h on regional roads, 100 km/h on national roads unless otherwise posted.

What is the pedestrian light sequence?
Green → amber → red → flashing amber → green.

What is the normal traffic-light sequence starting at green?
Green → amber → red → green.

What does a green light mean?
Proceed carefully, only if the road ahead is clear.

What do you check before starting the engine?
Handbrake on, gear in neutral, doors shut, seat and mirrors set correctly, and seatbelts secure.

Who has right of way at a junction of equal importance?
Traffic coming from your right, and vehicles already turning.

What should you do if an emergency vehicle approaches?
Stay calm, move aside only when safe and legal, then rejoin when clear.

What does a red traffic light mean?
Stop before the line; if you’re already in the junction turning right, complete the turn when safe.

How should you join a dual carriageway with a wide centre median?
Stop in the median when safe, then wait for a clear gap to fully join.

How should you join a dual carriageway with no median?
Wait on your side until you have a full, safe gap to cross.

If driving on a road with no centre line, where should you position the car?
Keep to the left as though a centre line existed.

What does an island in the centre of a crossing mean?
It separates the crossing into two — each side is treated independently.

What should you do if you break down on the motorway?
Pull onto the hard shoulder, turn on hazards, place a warning triangle if available, exit on the passenger side, stand behind the barrier, and contact Gardaí or motorway assistance.

What is a safe following distance?
1 metre per km/h or the two-second rule — double it on wet roads.

Why are STOP and YIELD signs shaped differently?
So they remain recognisable if covered in snow or damaged.

How do STOP and YIELD signs differ in shape?
STOP is octagonal; YIELD is an inverted triangle.

What are the hours when you must not use your horn in built-up areas?
Between 11:30 pm and 7:00 am except in emergencies.

What are the road markings for “No Entry”?
A continuous white line with a broken line behind it.

What should you do at a box junction?
Do not enter unless you can fully clear it; if turning right, you may wait on the box if you’re not obstructing traffic

Newcastle West – Longcourt House Hotel Free Driving Test Route

A turn-by-turn practice route from the Newcastle West – Longcourt House Hotel test centre. Follow it locally before test day.

From Newcastle West Longcourt House Hotel, pull out of Sheehan's Road and continue for about 180 metres

At the end of the road turn right onto Gortboy and follow it for roughly 300 metres before turning left into Kilcolman Drive

This estate is used for a short low-speed practice loop, so take your time with observations at each junction

Inside Kilcolman Drive, follow the road as it bends right and then left, and be ready to perform a U-turn manoeuvre when directed

After the turnaround, retrace your path through Kilcolman Drive, keeping right then left, and turn left back onto Gortboy

Drive along Gortboy for about 540 metres to the end of the road at the end of Gortboy

Turn right onto the N21 approach The Old Pike Roundabout and take it slight left

Exit left onto Rathina at the next mini roundabout on Rathina, take it left and exit left to continue on Rathina

Watch your speed and lane position on the busier N21 section turn left into Beechwood Gardens

Take a slight right into Beechwood Avenue, followed by a right into Meadow Court

In this cul-de-sac, complete a U-turn exit at the end of the road with a left back onto Beechwood Avenue

Turn left onto Rathina again, and at the end of the road turn left onto Saint Ita's Road

Follow Saint Ita's Road for about 270 metres turn right onto Bridge Street

Keep slight left on Bridge Street turn right into The Square in the town centre

Continue left through The Square, which becomes Church Street and then Churchtown Road

Take extra care with pedestrians and parked cars around the town

After Churchtown Road turn right onto Station Road and follow it for roughly 660 metres

Continue on Station Road turn right onto Gortboy, and finally take the 1st left back onto Sheehan's Road to return toward the Longcourt House Hotel test centre

Finish with a smooth, controlled park to complete the route

Where People Actually Fail in Newcastle West – Longcourt House Hotel

1. Observation on quiet roads

Because traffic is lighter, testers focus heavily on:

  • Mirror checks
  • Head movement
  • Awareness, not reaction

Common fail: Failing to check mirrors because "nothing was happening".

Quiet ≠ no observation.

2. Housing estates

Newcastle West – Longcourt House Hotel routes often include:

  • Tight residential estates
  • Parked cars
  • Speed ramps
  • Poor visibility at junctions

Testers expect:

  • Very low speed
  • Caution at every junction
  • Clear left–right checks

Common fail: rolling through estate junctions.

3. Junction positioning

Newcastle West – Longcourt House Hotel uses junctions that require:

  • Correct road positioning
  • Proper stopping when needed
  • Confident but safe pull-outs

Common fail:

  • Stopping too far back
  • Creeping forward without proper checks
  • Hesitation after committing

They watch decision quality, not speed.

4. Speed control (major issue)

This is one of the biggest fail reasons here.

Learners struggle with:

  • Staying too slow
  • Dropping speed unnecessarily
  • Not reaching speed limits when safe

Common fail: driving like you're scared instead of controlled.

Testers want appropriate speed, not caution overload.

5. Roundabouts and bends

Newcastle West – Longcourt House Hotel includes:

  • Smaller roundabouts
  • Curved approaches
  • Roads with limited forward visibility

Testers watch:

  • Early signalling
  • Lane discipline
  • Speed on approach

Common fail: entering roundabouts too fast or signalling late.

Where People Actually Fail in Cavan

1. Observation and awareness

Testers focus heavily on:

  • Mirror checks
  • Head movement
  • Constant awareness of surroundings

Common fail: Failing to check mirrors regularly because traffic seems light.

2. Junction positioning and decision-making

Cavan routes include various junction types that require:

  • Correct road positioning
  • Proper stopping when needed
  • Confident but safe pull-outs

Common fail: Hesitation or incorrect positioning at junctions.

3. Speed control

Learners struggle with:

  • Maintaining appropriate speed
  • Adapting to changing road conditions
  • Not driving too slowly or too fast

Common fail: Inconsistent speed control.

4. Roundabouts and complex intersections

Cavan includes:

  • Various roundabout sizes
  • Complex intersections
  • Multi-lane roads

Testers watch:

  • Early signalling
  • Lane discipline
  • Speed on approach

Common fail: Entering roundabouts incorrectly or signalling late.

What to Watch Out For in the Mulhuddart Driving Test

1. Estates, estates, estates

Mulhuddart is heavy on residential driving. Narrow roads, parked cars, bends, and constant hazards. Priority judgment matters — forcing through or being unsure both lose marks.

2. Observation at minor junctions

There are lots of small junctions with limited visibility. You must stop properly, edge out slowly, and show obvious head movement. Rolling stops are a common fail.

3. Speed control (easy to slip)

Low speed limits dominate. People drift over the limit without realising, especially after turns or downhill. Examiners are strict here.

4. Manoeuvres in tight spaces

Reverse around a corner or turnabout is very likely and often cramped. Kerbing, poor steering control, or missed blind-spot checks rack up faults fast.

5. Traffic flow & hesitation

Traffic can be stop-start. Over-hesitating at junctions or blocking traffic unnecessarily gets marked down. You need to be cautious but decisive.

6. Positioning on wider roads

Some routes show brief stretches of wider roads. Poor lane discipline or late positioning stands out immediately.

Bottom line

Mulhuddart fails people on observation, speed discipline, and estate driving judgment. It's not aggressive — it's technical. If your basics aren't automatic, the area exposes it quickly.

Wilton Driving Test Routes – What to Expect, Common Mistakes & Local Tips

1. Heavy traffic & pressure driving

Wilton is busy. Hospital traffic, buses, pedestrians, and constant movement mean you're tested on decision-making under pressure. Hesitation or blocking traffic gets marked.

2. Big junctions & lane discipline

Multi-lane junctions and roundabouts are common. Late lane choice, drifting lanes, or weak positioning shows immediately.

3. Speed control (very easy to slip)

Speed limits change quickly between residential roads and main routes. Creeping over — especially downhill or after junctions — racks up faults fast.

4. Observation at junctions

There are several awkward or busy junctions. Full stops where required, clear head movement, and clean pull-outs matter. Guessing gets punished.

5. Manoeuvres in real traffic

Reverse around a corner or turnabout is likely and often done where traffic is nearby. Poor observation or kerbing stands out fast.

6. Pedestrians & cyclists

Between the hospital and nearby estates, vulnerable road users are everywhere. Missing one is a serious fault.

Bottom line

Wilton fails people on traffic management, lane discipline, and observation under pressure. You don't pass by being slow — you pass by being calm, decisive, and precise.

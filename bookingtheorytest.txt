✅ THE FINAL THEORY TEST CHECKLIST (IRELAND)

Everything you need to do, in the exact order you actually do it.

1. Confirm you’re eligible

You must be 16+ for most licence categories.

Have some form of ID ready (passport or public services card).

2. Gather the documents + info you’ll need

You need these before you even try to book:

Valid email address (you’ll get all confirmations here).

Valid phone number (optional but useful for reminders).

Payment method (debit/credit card).

Category you’re sitting (usually B for cars).

Your PPS number (very often required).

3. Create / log into your MyRoadSafety account

Go to https://www.myrsa.ie/

Register or log in.

Verify your email if needed.

4. Find a test centre + choose your date/time

Inside the portal:

Select Book Theory Test.

Choose your county and test centre.

Look at available dates — pick the earliest that suits you.

Confirm the slot.

5. Pay for the test

Standard car theory test: €45 (price can change but this is the usual).

Pay by card.

Wait for the booking confirmation email.

What that email MUST contain:

Test centre address

Date & time

Reference number

Rules & what to bring

If this email doesn’t arrive → check spam → contact support.

6. Start preparation (do NOT skip this)

To actually be prepared:

Official RSA Questions & Answers (the exact bank used in the real test).

Practice hazard perception (if your category needs it).

Practice mock exams until you consistently score 90%+.

This is where your DriveFlow-style prep system fits in.

7. Know what ID you must bring

Accepted ID for Ireland usually includes:

Irish passport

Irish Public Services Card

Irish Residence Permit

Bring the exact ID that matches the details used during booking.

8. Understand test-day rules

These are non-negotiable:

Arrive 15 minutes early.

No phones or smartwatches inside.

No notes, no study materials at the desk.

You must sign in and confirm your identity.

9. Double-check logistics the day before

This is where most people mess up. Tick all of this:

✔ Know exactly where the test centre is

✔ Check traffic / bus schedules

✔ Lay out your ID

✔ Check your booking confirmation email again

✔ Sleep properly — stupid mistakes cost easy marks

10. On the day

Arrive early

Sign in

Locker your belongings

Do the practice questions on the screen

Take your time — you have more than enough

Review before submitting

11. After the test

You get your result instantly.

If you pass → you’ll receive your Theory Test Certificate.

If you fail → you can re-book immediately.

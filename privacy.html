Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 February 2026

DriveFlow ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use www.driveflow.ie.

We may collect the following information:

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data based on:

  • Your consent

4. Sharing of Data

We may share data with:

  • Payment processors (e.g. Stripe)
  • Analytics providers (e.g. Google Analytics)
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers

All third parties are required to handle your data securely and lawfully.

5. Data Retention

We only retain personal data for as long as necessary to provide services or comply with legal obligations.

6. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise your rights, contact us via the website.

7. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, but no system is 100% secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the site means you accept the updated policy.

🔧 What You MUST Know (Bonnet Check)

They want to see that you can identify each component and explain its purpose + how to check levels. You DO NOT need to touch anything.

1. Engine Oil

Location: Dipstick (usually yellow/orange).

Check: Pull out, wipe, dip again, level is between MIN and MAX.

Why: Lubricates engine.

2. Coolant

Location: Translucent tank with coloured liquid.

Check: Level between MIN and MAX when engine is cold.

Why: Stops engine overheating.

3. Brake Fluid

Location: Small reservoir near the back of engine bay.

Check: Level between MIN and MAX.

Why: Lets brakes work properly.

4. Power Steering Fluid (if hydraulic, many new cars are electric so may not have this)

Check: Level between MIN and MAX.

What to say if electric steering: “This car has electronic power steering — so no fluid.”

5. Windshield Washer Fluid

Location: Blue cap usually.

Check: Visual check / top up as required.

Why: Clear visibility.

6. Engine Coolant Fan / Belts (if asked)

Check: Belts shouldn’t be torn or loose.

Trick: They rarely ask this.

🔥 Tips & Tricks (What Testers Actually Like)

Say what you see. If you forget, just point and describe its job.

Confidence > perfection. They don’t need exact colour or part names.

Memorise the “Big 3”: Oil, Coolant, Brake Fluid — these matter most.

If unsure about power steering: “Some cars use electronic steering; if hydraulic, I’d check the fluid is between min and max.”

Washer fluid is an easy win — don’t overlook it.

Don’t touch a hot engine. If they ask, say:
“You only check levels when the engine is cold.”

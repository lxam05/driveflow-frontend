Hand Signals

Master the hand signals required for your driving test

1. Turning Left

"I'm turning left."
Front View
How to do it: Put your left arm straight out horizontally through the window.
Position: Hand flat, palm facing forward.
Remember: Easy. Clean. Straight line.

2. Turning Right

"I'm turning right."
Front View
Option 1: Stick your right arm straight out.
Option 2 (RHD cars): If you can't use the right arm out the window (Irish cars are RHD), raise your left arm up, elbow bent, palm facing forward.

3. Slowing Down / Stopping

"I'm slowing or stopping."
Front View
How to do it: Put your right arm out and move it up and down like you're patting a big dog.
Movement: Steady, clear movement — not frantic.

4. Overtaking / Passing

"I'm letting you know I'm overtaking."
Front View
How to do it: Extend your right arm out, make a forward sweeping motion.
Note: You won't use this often, but you must know it.

5. Cyclist Hand Signals

Testers sometimes mix car + cyclist questions.

  • Left turn: Left arm straight out.
  • Right turn: Right arm straight out.
  • Slowing: One hand out, palm down, moving up and down.

Exact same principles — simpler because cyclists don't use windows.

6. How the Tester Checks You

Describe the Signal

They'll ask you to describe a signal ("Show me how you'd signal slowing down")

Physically Demonstrate

They'll ask you to physically do them in the seat

Key Point

Do them confidently. Hesitation kills marks.

7. Quick Tip to Remember Them

  • Straight arm = direction.
  • Bent arm up = right turn (if you can't use the right window).
  • Up-and-down = slowing.
  • Forward sweep = overtaking.

If you can picture these four moves, you're sorted.

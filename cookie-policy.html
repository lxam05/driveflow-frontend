Cookie Policy

Last updated: 10 February 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how DriveFlow uses cookies on www.driveflow.ie.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites function and collect usage information.

2. Cookies We Use

We use:

  • Essential cookies – required for basic site functionality
  • Analytics cookies – to understand how users interact with the site (e.g. Google Analytics)
  • Payment/session cookies – to manage purchases and secure access

3. Why We Use Cookies

Cookies help us:

  • Keep the site working properly
  • Improve performance and usability
  • Understand which pages and routes are most useful

4. Managing Cookies

You can:

  • Disable cookies in your browser settings
  • Delete existing cookies at any time

Note: disabling cookies may affect site functionality.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services we use (such as analytics and payment providers).

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as required. Continued use of the site indicates acceptance.

Cookie Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how DriveFlow uses cookies and similar technologies on www.driveflow.ie only. The DriveFlow mobile app does not use browser cookies. For the full picture of how we process personal data, see our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites function and, where permitted, collect usage information. Similar technologies (such as local storage) may be used for the same purposes.

2. Cookies We Use

We use:

  • Essential cookies / storage — required for basic site functionality, such as keeping you signed in and completing purchases. These do not require consent.
  • Analytics cookies — Google Analytics, used to understand how visitors use the site. These are non-essential and are only used if you Accept.
  • Advertising cookies — Google Ads measurement tags, used for advertising measurement. These are non-essential and are only used if you Accept.

3. Consent

Non-essential analytics and advertising cookies are not set by default. When you visit the website, you will see a cookie banner asking you to Accept or Reject non-essential cookies.

  • Accept — we may load Google Analytics and Google Ads tags and set related cookies.
  • Reject — we will not load those non-essential tags for analytics/advertising purposes.

Your choice is stored locally in your browser so we can remember it on later visits. Continued browsing without accepting is not treated as consent for non-essential cookies.

4. Why We Use Cookies

Cookies and similar technologies help us:

  • Keep the site working properly (essential)
  • Manage secure access and purchases (essential)
  • Understand which pages and routes are most useful (analytics — with consent)
  • Measure advertising performance (advertising — with consent)

5. Managing Cookies

You can:

  • Use the on-site cookie banner to Accept or Reject non-essential cookies
  • Clear site data in your browser to reset your choice and see the banner again
  • Control or delete cookies through your browser settings

Note: disabling essential cookies or storage may affect sign-in and purchase functionality.

6. Third-Party Cookies

If you Accept, third-party services such as Google Analytics and Google Ads may set cookies. Payment providers (for example Stripe) may also use cookies or similar technologies as part of the checkout process. Those parties process data under their own policies.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date on this page. Please review it periodically.

Best driving test centre in Galway?

Updated July 2026 · Written by Liam O'Connor · CSO / RSA ROA data

Galway learners can choose city-fringe centres or quieter county options. Pass rates and waits vary widely — Clifden often looks strong on paper, while city centres trade a tougher road environment for shorter travel.

On the latest CSO numbers, Clifden shows the strongest pass-rate / wait combination among Galway centres with full data (68.5% pass, 13 weeks to invite). That is still not a guarantee — local road difficulty and your preparation matter more than the table.

How we source this: Source: CSO / RSA driving test statistics (ROA), Category B, July 2026. “Best” here means highest published Category B pass rate, then shorter invite wait — not an RSA endorsement.

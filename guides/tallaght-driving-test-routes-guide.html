Tallaght Driving Test Routes: Roads and Areas to Practice

A local guide to the roads, roundabouts, and estates you should practice before sitting your RSA test at the Tallaght test centre.

The Tallaght test centre sits in one of the busier corners of south Dublin, and the routes examiners use reflect that. You'll deal with dual carriageways, multi-lane roundabouts, tight housing estates, and traffic-heavy junctions, often within the same 30 minute test. Knowing the local road network before your test date takes a huge amount of pressure off, especially when you're already nervous.

This guide walks you through the typical corridors examiners draw from around Tallaght, the pressure points to focus on, and how to structure your practice sessions so nothing on test day feels unfamiliar.

Where the Tallaght test centre is

The centre is based in the Cookstown Industrial Estate area, close to the Cookstown Way and within easy reach of the N81, the Belgard Road, and the Tallaght bypass. That location matters because examiners can send you in almost any direction: toward Jobstown, up toward Firhouse, across to Kingswood, or down along the Greenhills Road corridor. Practicing only one side of the centre is a common mistake.

Typical route corridors around Tallaght

The RSA does not publish official routes, but based on commonly reported patterns from learners and instructors, tests from Tallaght tend to cover a mix of the following areas.

Cookstown and Belgard Road

Expect to leave the centre onto local industrial estate roads before joining Belgard Road. Lane discipline here is critical because the road has multiple lanes feeding roundabouts and traffic light junctions in quick succession. Learners often get caught in the wrong lane approaching the Belgard roundabout or the junction near the Luas line.

The N81 and Jobstown

Some routes head out along the N81 toward Jobstown, Fettercairn, or Killinarden. This section tests your ability to hold speed on a national road, judge safe gaps for lane changes, and handle busy signalised junctions. The area around Jobstown has residential estates with narrow entries that examiners like for reverse-around-a-corner manoeuvres.

Firhouse, Old Bawn, and Bohernabreena

Eastbound routes may take you through Old Bawn Road toward Firhouse. This corridor has a mix of 50 km/h and 60 km/h zones, school areas, and pedestrian crossings. The Firhouse Road itself has fast-changing conditions with parked cars and cyclists to watch for.

Kingswood, Kilnamanagh, and Greenhills

West and north of the centre, you'll find quieter residential areas that are ideal for testing your estate driving, junction observation, and turnabouts. Kingswood and Kilnamanagh in particular have the kind of quiet cul-de-sacs and T-junctions examiners use for manoeuvres.

Roundabouts you should know

Tallaght is roundabout-heavy, and this is where a lot of learners lose marks. Focus your practice on:

  • The Belgard roundabout, with Luas tracks and multi-lane approaches
  • Roundabouts along the Tallaght bypass feeding the N81
  • Smaller estate roundabouts around Kingswood and Fettercairn
  • The junction network around the Square and Cookstown Way

Practice lane selection on approach, correct signalling on exit, and confident mirror checks. Hesitation on a busy Tallaght roundabout is one of the fastest ways to pick up a grade 2 fault.

Common pressure points on test day

  • Lane discipline on Belgard Road and around The Square
  • Merging onto the N81 and holding a steady speed
  • Pedestrian crossings near shopping areas and the Luas stops
  • Cyclists and buses on Greenhills Road and Firhouse Road
  • Estate observation, particularly clear head checks at unmarked junctions
  • Manoeuvres in tight residential streets with parked cars nearby

How to prepare for a Tallaght test

Structure your pre-test lessons around the four corridors above. Aim for at least two practice sessions in each direction so you're comfortable regardless of which way the examiner turns you at the centre gate. Do a full mock test with no prompts from your instructor in the final week, and finish with a session focused purely on roundabouts and lane changes.

Before you go in, review your car checks, know your RSA secondary questions, and make sure your tax, insurance, and NCT are all in order. The examiner will not proceed without them.

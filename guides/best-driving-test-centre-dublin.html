Best driving test centre in Dublin?

Updated July 2026 · Written by Liam O'Connor · CSO / RSA ROA data

Dublin centres sit on some of the busiest test roads in Ireland. Pass rates are often below the national average, and waits can stretch — so picking a centre is really about matching roads you can practise with a wait you can live with.

On the latest CSO numbers, Raheny shows the strongest pass-rate / wait combination among Dublin centres with full data (51.8% pass, 29 weeks to invite). That is still not a guarantee — local road difficulty and your preparation matter more than the table.

How we source this: Source: CSO / RSA driving test statistics (ROA), Category B, July 2026. “Best” here means highest published Category B pass rate, then shorter invite wait — not an RSA endorsement.

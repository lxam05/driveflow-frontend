Carrick-on-Shannon Free Driving Test Route

A turn-by-turn practice route from the Carrick-on-Shannon test centre. Follow it locally before test day.

From Carrick-on-Shannon Test Centre depart Landmark Court and take the 1st left.

Take the next left, then another left onto the local road leading towards the Dublin Road.

Turn left onto the Dublin Road.

Take the next right onto Main Street and watch for pedestrians and parked vehicles.

Take a slight right onto the L33982 and follow it for around 800 metres out of the town.

Turn left into Shannon Grove and continue along the road.

Perform a turnabout when directed by the examiner, using full observations.

Leave Shannon Grove by turning right, then right again.

Turn left back onto the local road.

At the end of the road turn right onto the L3400.

Take the next right onto Leitrim Road.

At the end of the road turn left onto the Dublin Road.

Take the slight left fork onto Summerhill.

Take a slight left onto the L3412.

Turn left into Oaklands and follow Oaklands Manor around.

Keep right, then right again, then left through the estate.

Be ready for a stop or hill start in this 50 km/h residential area.

At the end of the road turn left and follow the road out of the estate.

Turn left onto the unnamed link road.

Turn right onto Castlecara Road and continue for over a kilometre.

At the roundabout take the 2nd exit to rejoin the Dublin Road.

At the next roundabout take the slight left onto the Carrick-on-Shannon Bypass Road and exit left.

Shortly after, turn right onto the unnamed access road.

At the end of the road turn right back into Landmark Court.

Return to the Carrick-on-Shannon Test Centre where the examiner will complete your debrief.

Why Carrick-on-Shannon Catches People Out

Carrick-on-Shannon looks easy on paper. That's the trap.

These practice routes are designed to help learner drivers become familiar with the roads, junctions, estates, and traffic conditions commonly used in the Carrick-on-Shannon driving test. Carrick-on-Shannon is known for quiet roads mixed with sudden decision points — which is exactly where people fail.

Where People Actually Fail in Carrick-on-Shannon

1. Observation on quiet roads

Because traffic is lighter, testers focus heavily on:

  • Mirror checks
  • Head movement
  • Awareness, not reaction

Common fail: Failing to check mirrors because "nothing was happening".

Quiet ≠ no observation.

2. Housing estates

Carrick-on-Shannon routes often include:

  • Tight residential estates
  • Parked cars
  • Speed ramps
  • Poor visibility at junctions

Testers expect:

  • Very low speed
  • Caution at every junction
  • Clear left–right checks

Common fail: rolling through estate junctions.

3. Junction positioning

Carrick-on-Shannon uses junctions that require:

  • Correct road positioning
  • Proper stopping when needed
  • Confident but safe pull-outs

Common fail:

  • Stopping too far back
  • Creeping forward without proper checks
  • Hesitation after committing

They watch decision quality, not speed.

4. Speed control (major issue)

This is one of the biggest fail reasons here.

Learners struggle with:

  • Staying too slow
  • Dropping speed unnecessarily
  • Not reaching speed limits when safe

Common fail: driving like you're scared instead of controlled.

Testers want appropriate speed, not caution overload.

5. Roundabouts and bends

Carrick-on-Shannon includes:

  • Smaller roundabouts
  • Curved approaches
  • Roads with limited forward visibility

Testers watch:

  • Early signalling
  • Lane discipline
  • Speed on approach

Common fail: entering roundabouts too fast or signalling late.

Why So Many Irish Learners Fail Their Driving Test

The RSA reports that over 50% of Irish learner drivers fail their driving test each year. The most common reasons aren't poor driving — they're poor preparation:

  • Unfamiliar junctions and roundabouts
  • Not knowing which lane to be in
  • Nerves caused by driving in an unknown area
  • Hesitation at busy or complex intersections
  • Incorrect positioning on roads they've never driven before

If you've already driven the test area ten times before your test day, none of these feel like problems. That's what DriveFlow is designed to do.

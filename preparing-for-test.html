Preparing for Test Day

Master the skills that matter and get test-ready

⭐ THE PLAN THAT GETS YOU TO A PASS

Even with 2–3 hours of car time a week.
Short. Punchy. Zero bullshit.
If you follow this, you will drive test-ready.

🔥 WHAT ACTUALLY MATTERS (80% OF THE TEST)

▼

Master these → you pass.

  • Mirrors (obsessively)
  • Observation at junctions
  • Positioning
  • Indications (on & off)
  • Smooth clutch control
  • Roundabout discipline
  • Not hesitating like a zombie

🚀 WEEKLY STRUCTURE (3–4 HOURS TOTAL)

▼

1️⃣ Skills Session (60–75 min)

▼

Where you build the foundations.

You drill:

  • Mirrors every 8–10 seconds
  • MSMPSL until automatic
  • Smooth moving off & stopping
  • Clutch control for crawling, corners, and junction creep
  • Hill starts with zero rollback
Goal:

Everything must look calm and intentional.

2️⃣ Manoeuvres Session (60–90 min)

▼

Predictable. Easy marks if you practise correctly.

You must master:

  • ✔ Reverse Around a Corner

    Creep. 1.5–2 ft from kerb. Relentless blind-spot checks. No swinging wide.

  • ✔ Turnabout (3-Point Turn)

    Look both shoulders every time you reverse. Slow steering. Do NOT hit kerb. Do NOT panic-steer.

  • ✔ Hill start

    For Manual Transmissions only. Practice getting your biting point while the handbrake is on, then releasing it.

  • Note: Take as much time as you need during these manoeuvres, there is no time limit. Observations are what's really being tested here.

3️⃣ Test Simulation Drive (45–60 min)

▼

Exactly like the real test. No chatting. No mercy.

  • Mirrors like a psycho
  • Clean junctions
  • Solid lane discipline
  • Good roundabout calls
  • Indicate with purpose
  • Cancel indicators
  • Pull in / move off repeatedly
  • No hesitation unless it's actually dangerous
Goal:

0 majors, <6 minors every run.

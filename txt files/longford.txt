5. Roundabout Behaviour at This Centre

Roundabouts are one of the main assessment tools in Longford.

Large / Multi-Lane Roundabouts (N4 Area)

The N4 roundabouts are where many learners lose marks.

Examiners watch for:

Early lane selection

Clear signalling

Mirror checks before changing lanes

Smooth approach speed

Common mistakes:

Drifting lanes

Late decisions

Entering too fast

Failing to commit when a safe gap appears

Hesitation here can cost marks just as much as rushing.

Smaller Residential Roundabouts

Found around estates and quieter roads.

Learners often:

Skip proper observation

Fail to signal on exit

Approach too quickly

Even small roundabouts are treated seriously.

6. Junction Types That Cause Fails
Hidden Estate Junctions

Abbeycartron and similar estates have:

Parked cars

Narrow roads

Limited visibility

You must stop properly and creep forward. Rolling stops are common fail points.

Fast Approach Junctions

On roads like Athlone Road or Granard Road.

Mistakes include:

Braking too late

Forgetting mirrors before slowing

Poor positioning before turning

Speed must be adjusted early, not at the line.

Offset T-Junctions

These require strong positioning and observation.

Examiners watch:

Shoulder checks

Steering control

Lane discipline when emerging

Junctions After Bends

Longford has several bends leading into junctions.

You’re expected to anticipate, not react at the last second.

7. Speed Limit Traps in Longford
N4 Transitions

Speed changes between 100km/h, 80km/h and 60km/h zones.

Learners often:

Don’t adjust smoothly

Brake harshly

Miss signs entirely

Examiners notice immediately.

Town Centre Roads

Main Street looks wide but is strictly 50km/h.

Going even slightly over consistently will cost marks.

Residential Estates

You must reduce speed significantly.

Driving at 45–50km/h through estates shows poor hazard awareness.

School Areas

Be extra alert near schools. Examiners expect visible caution.

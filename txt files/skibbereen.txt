Junction Types That Cause Fails
1. Hidden Residential Junctions
Many estate exits have:
Parked cars
Bushes or walls blocking view
Learners fail by:
Not creeping
Rolling forward without full stop
Rushing out
2. Rural-to-Town Junctions
Approach roads into Skibbereen transition quickly from 80km/h to 50km/h.
Mistakes include:
Late braking
Poor mirror checks
Misjudging town traffic flow
3. Offset T-Junctions
Common near older housing areas.
Learners:
Position incorrectly
Miss shoulder checks
Drift wide on turns
4. Bends Before Junctions
Some rural junctions appear just after bends.
You must reduce speed early and set up properly.

⭐ THE PLAN THAT GETS YOU TO A PASS — EVEN WITH 2–3 HOURS OF CAR TIME A WEEK

Short. Punchy. Zero bullshit.
If you follow this, you will drive test-ready.

🔥 WHAT ACTUALLY MATTERS (80% OF THE TEST):

Mirrors (obsessively)

Observation at junctions

Positioning

Indications (on & off)

Smooth clutch control

Roundabout discipline

Not hesitating like a zombie

Master those → you pass.

🚀 WEEKLY STRUCTURE (3–4 HOURS TOTAL)
1️⃣ Skills Session (60–75 min)

Where you build the foundations.

You drill:

Mirrors every 8–10 seconds

MSMPSL until automatic

Smooth moving off & stopping

Clutch control for crawling, corners, and junction creep

Hill starts with zero rollback

Everything must look calm and intentional.

2️⃣ Manoeuvres Session (60–90 min)

Predictable. Easy marks if you practise correctly.

You must master:
✔ Reverse Around a Corner

Creep.
1.5–2 ft from kerb.
Relentless blind-spot checks.
No swinging wide.

✔ Turnabout (3-Point Turn)

Look both shoulders every time you reverse.
Slow steering.
Do NOT hit kerb.
Do NOT panic-steer.

3️⃣ Test Simulation Drive (45–60 min)

Exactly like the real test. No chatting. No mercy.

Mirrors like a psycho

Clean junctions

Solid lane discipline

Good roundabout calls

Indicate with purpose

Cancel indicators

Pull in / move off repeatedly

No hesitation unless it’s actually dangerous

Goal: 0 majors, <6 minors every run.

🔥 DAILY NO-CAR TRAINING (THIS MAKES UP FOR LIMITED CAR TIME)
1️⃣ Mirror/Observation Timing (30 sec bursts all day)

Centre → Right → Left
Repeat until it’s instinct.

2️⃣ Roundabout Judgment Training

Watch Irish dashcam videos.
Pause → decide your lane + your gap.
This builds real test decision-making.

3️⃣ Study Your Test Routes

Google Maps = your secret weapon.
Know:

Roundabout markings

Speed zones

Tight lefts

Dodgy yields

Knowing what’s coming saves you nerves.

4️⃣ Bonnet Checks + Car Controls

You MUST know these instantly:

Oil

Coolant

Brake fluid

Washer fluid

Dipstick
Plus: demister, fog lights, hazards, beams.

This takes 20 mins and impresses examiners big time.

How Test Routes Work at This Centre

There are no fixed routes in Kilkenny.

Instead, examiners reuse the same road networks, mixing them differently on each test. Most routes follow looping patterns that include:

Leaving the Government Buildings area

Urban driving through built-up streets

One or two major roundabouts

Residential estates for manoeuvres

A stretch of faster road or ring road

A return through city traffic

Learning the geography matters more than memorising directions. If you understand how traffic flows around Kilkenny, the test feels predictable instead of stressful.

Roundabout Behaviour at Kilkenny

Roundabouts are a major feature of this test centre.

Larger / Multi-Lane Roundabouts

These are often used on the ring road and main approaches.

Examiners closely watch:

Lane choice before entry

Early and correct signalling

Mirror checks before changing lanes

Speed control on approach

Common mistakes include:

Choosing the wrong lane too late

Indicating late or not at all

Drifting between lanes

Hesitating and causing traffic to slow

You’re expected to commit confidently once the gap is safe.

Smaller Residential Roundabouts

Found in estates and quieter areas.

Learners often:

Stop unnecessarily

Fail to scan properly

Rush exits without mirrors

Treat these exactly like bigger roundabouts — full observation still applies.

Junction Types That Cause Fails
Hidden Residential Junctions

Many estate exits around Kilkenny have:

High walls

Parked cars

Poor sightlines

Learners fail by rolling forward without stopping or not creeping to gain a proper view.

Fast Approach Junctions

On roads like Callan Road, junctions appear after faster stretches.

Examiners look for:

Early speed reduction

Proper mirror checks

Controlled entry

Braking late here is a common serious fault.

Offset T-Junctions

Common in older residential areas.

Mistakes include:

Cutting corners

Poor positioning

Missing shoulder checks

Bends Before Junctions

Several junctions appear immediately after bends on approach roads.

You must slow early and set up before the bend, not after it.

Speed Limit Traps Around Kilkenny
Town Entry Zones

Speed limits drop quickly when approaching the city.

Learners often fail by:

Staying at 60km/h too long

Braking suddenly instead of progressively

Wide Urban Roads

Some city roads feel fast but remain 50km/h.

Examiners expect precise speed control, not “around the limit”.

School Zones

Kilkenny has several schools near common routes.

Any speeding or poor anticipation here is taken very seriously.

Residential Estates

Speed must drop significantly. Coasting at town-road speed inside estates is a common fault.

Kilkenny is unforgiving of sloppy habits.

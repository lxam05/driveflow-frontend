Theory Test Pass Checklist

Everything you need to know to pass your Irish theory test

What to Bring

Valid ID

Bring a valid form of identification (passport, driving license, or national ID card)

Booking Confirmation

Print or have your booking confirmation email ready on your phone

Arrive Early

Arrive at least 15 minutes before your scheduled test time

Test Requirements

Number of Questions

You will answer 40 multiple-choice questions

Passing Score

You need to answer at least 35 questions correctly (87.5%) to pass

Time Allowed

You have 45 minutes to complete the test

Question Navigation

You cannot go back to previous questions once answered (like the real exam)

Tips to Pass First Time

  • Practice regularly using our mock tests and topic practice questions
  • Study road signs thoroughly - they appear frequently in the test
  • Understand stopping distances for different speeds and conditions
  • Learn the rules of the road, especially right of way and roundabouts
  • Take multiple mock tests to get familiar with the format and timing
  • Read each question carefully - don't rush
  • If unsure, eliminate obviously wrong answers first
  • Get a good night's sleep before your test
  • Stay calm and manage your time - you have plenty of time for 40 questions
  • Review your weak areas using our topic-specific practice

Test Day Checklist

Before Leaving Home

✓ Check you have your ID and booking confirmation
✓ Plan your route to the test centre
✓ Leave with plenty of time to spare

At the Test Centre

✓ Arrive 15 minutes early
✓ Follow instructions from staff
✓ Use the bathroom before starting
✓ Turn off your phone

Remember

You can retake the theory test if you don't pass. Use your results to identify areas for improvement and practice more before booking again.

Donegal Free Driving Test Route

A turn-by-turn practice route from the Donegal test centre. Follow it locally before test day.

From Donegal Test Centre depart onto Donegal ED and continue to the end of the road.

Turn right onto Ballybofey Road and follow it for about 770 metres.

Continue straight ahead as it becomes Upper Main Street, staying with the flow of traffic through the town.

After roughly 500 metres take the next right onto Water Street.

Continue straight as the road becomes New Row and then Tirconnel Street.

At the end of the road turn right onto Blue Stack Drive.

Take the 1st right into Orchard Drive and pull in for a short stop.

Move off on Orchard Drive and perform a controlled U-turn where safe.

Head back to the end of Blue Stack Drive and turn left onto Tirconnel Street.

Keep right to stay on Tirconnel Street.

At the end of the road turn left onto The Diamond.

Keep right to follow The Diamond around.

Take a slight left onto Quay Street.

Take the next left into The Glebe.

Keep right to stay on The Glebe.

Turn left onto Glebe Crest.

Perform a U-turn on Glebe Crest and pull in briefly to demonstrate the manoeuvre.

Depart Glebe Crest and turn right back onto The Glebe.

Keep left to follow The Glebe.

Turn left onto Quay Street and stay on it for about 1.4 kilometres.

Turn left onto Rarooey Road and pull in on the right for a short stop.

Move off and continue along Rarooey Road for around 1.4 kilometres.

Turn left to remain on Rarooey Road.

Go straight onto the unnamed road.

Turn left onto Old Laghey Road.

Keep slight right at the fork.

Turn right onto Ballybofey Road.

Take the left back into Donegal ED to arrive at the test centre.

About the Drumlonagher test centre

The RSA driving test centre for this area is located at the Donegal Public Services Centre in Drumlonagher, approximately 1.5 km east of Donegal Town centre. The Eircode is F94 DK6C.

It serves a wide catchment area covering Donegal Town, Ballyshannon, Laghey, Mountcharles, Inver, Killybegs direction commuters, and anyone using this centre instead of Letterkenny or Buncrana.

The driving environment here is a genuine mix of:

  • The N15 east towards Barnesmore and Ballybofey
  • The N56 loop around the north and west of town
  • The R267 / Derry Road through town
  • Residential estates around Milltown, Ardeskin and Drumcliff
  • Donegal Town centre, including the Diamond
  • The Drumlonagher roundabout right outside the centre

How test routes work at this centre

There is no single fixed route. Examiners choose from a network of roads they know well and loop back through the same road types depending on time and traffic.

What helps most is knowing the road network well enough that no junction or road type comes as a surprise. You'll nearly always encounter the Drumlonagher roundabout, some estate work, a national road section and some town driving.

Core areas examiners frequently use

  • The N15 east of Drumlonagher towards Barnesmore and Ballybofey direction
  • The N56 around the north side of town towards Mountcharles and Killybegs direction
  • The R267 / Derry Road through Donegal Town
  • Donegal Town centre and the Diamond
  • Milltown Road and the estates off it
  • Ardeskin and surrounding residential streets
  • Drumcliff Road and Drumcliff Hill approach
  • The Donegal Shopping Centre area and approach from the bypass
  • Laghey direction on the N15 south, including the junction at Laghey village

Roundabout behaviour

The Drumlonagher roundabout

This is the main roundabout and it's right outside the test centre. You'll use it at the start and end of the test, and possibly in between as well.

What examiners watch for here:

  • Proper approach speed
  • Correct lane selection before the roundabout
  • Yielding to traffic already on the roundabout
  • Signalling correctly when exiting
  • Proper observation before pulling out

Common mistakes here include under-reading the approach speed of vehicles already on the roundabout, exiting without a left signal, and poor mirror checks after exit.

Junction types that cause fails

  • T-junctions with limited visibility in estates off Milltown Road and Ardeskin
  • Fast approach roads joining the N15, where gap judgement matters
  • The Diamond area junctions, with pedestrians, priority decisions and tight positioning
  • Estate exits onto busier roads, where a quick glance is not enough

Speed limit traps

  • The N15 east of Drumlonagher drops from 80 km/h to 60 km/h and then 50 km/h approaching built-up areas
  • The approach from the N15 into Donegal Town proper catches learners carrying bypass speed too long
  • Residential estates in Ardeskin and Milltown vary between 30 km/h and 50 km/h
  • The R267 past Donegal District Hospital needs careful speed and observation, especially in morning slots
  • Roads around the shopping centre can look faster than the posted limit

Common mistakes at this centre

  • Observation at the Drumlonagher roundabout
  • Missing mirror checks before and after turns
  • Hesitation at N15 junctions
  • Poor positioning around the Diamond
  • Late or missing signals on estate exits
  • Not adjusting speed early enough when entering residential areas
  • Incomplete observation at estate exits
  • Forgetting to signal left when leaving roundabouts

Examiner behaviour patterns

  • They are strict on junction observation
  • They are patient with hesitation if the observation is clearly there
  • Positioning errors, especially on left turns, are consistently marked
  • Mirror checks are monitored closely
  • Instructions are normally given in good time, but they expect you to listen and prepare early

The overall feedback is that this centre is fair. The pass rate sits around 61-62%, but preparation still matters a lot.

High-failure hotspots

  • Drumlonagher roundabout for observation and signalling faults
  • The Diamond and town centre approach for positioning and pedestrian awareness
  • N15 east for speed and lane control under pressure
  • Estate exits in Ardeskin and Milltown for observation and decision-making
  • R267 / Derry Road for speed and awareness around crossings and junctions

Test day flow at this centre

Leaving the centre: you'll pull out of the Public Services Centre car park and almost immediately face the Drumlonagher roundabout.

Early test: you'll usually head either towards the N15 east or into town via the R267, so mirrors, speed changes and junction behaviour are assessed quickly.

Mid-test: this is often when estate work and manoeuvres happen on quieter residential streets.

Final section: routes usually loop back towards Drumlonagher, and learners often relax too early. The examiner is still marking right up to the final park.

Local preparation tips

  • Drive the Drumlonagher roundabout from all three main approaches until it feels automatic
  • Practise through the Diamond in normal traffic, not only when it's quiet
  • Get comfortable on the N15 east at national-road speeds
  • Practise the estates in Ardeskin and Milltown Road
  • Do pre-test lessons with a local instructor who knows the specific roads used here
  • Avoid relying on memorised directions - focus on road type, positioning and observation

Frequently asked questions

How long is the driving test at Drumlonagher?

The driving portion is typically around 30 minutes, covering at least 7.5 km, plus a short pre-drive check at the start.

Are routes fixed at this centre?

No. The RSA does not publish fixed routes, but the same road network is reused regularly.

Can I practise realistic routes before my test?

Yes. Pay particular attention to the Drumlonagher roundabout, the N15 east of town, and the estate roads off Milltown Road and Ardeskin.

Is the Drumlonagher centre considered hard?

Not especially. It's varied rather than brutal, and learners who know the geography usually find it manageable.

What is the best way to prepare for this centre?

Know the roundabout, know the speed transitions, know the estates, and do a few pre-test lessons with a Donegal-based instructor who tests here regularly.
